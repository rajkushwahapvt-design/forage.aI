# 5-Minute Demo Video Script (Concise)

> Open this link in Chrome and follow this script while recording your screen.

---

### [0:00 – 0:45] Intro & The Golden Rule
* **Action:** Show the landing page. Point to the intake fields.
* **Speech:**  
  > *"Hi team Websites.co.in! I'm Raj Kushwaha, presenting 'AI Builds My Website'. A business owner inputs what they have—links, Google Maps, or price lists—and the app builds a production-ready website. Our fundamental principle is the **Golden Rule: Never invent facts**. If an address or price isn't verified in the data, we never hallucinate—we prompt the user. Let's see this in action."*

---

### [0:45 – 2:15] Business 1: Artisan Bakery & Section Regeneration
* **Action:** Click **"Artisan Bakery & Cafe"**, then click **"Build My Website"**.
* **Speech:**  
  > *"First, we load Sharma & Sons Bakery in Mumbai with their Instagram, Google listing, and uploaded menu. The engine extracts verified prices—like Sourdough at ₹280 and Belgian Cake at ₹950—along with hours and real reviews.  
  > In the studio, we can regenerate any section individually without breaking the site. I'll click 'Regenerate' on the Hero block, ask it to 'emphasize fresh morning sourdough', and apply. The copy updates instantly while preserving the rest of the site."*

### [2:15 – 3:30] Business 2: Home Services & Edge Cases
* **Action:** Return to Intake, click **"HVAC & Home Services"**, then click **"Build My Website"**.
* **Speech:**  
  > *"Next is Apex Plumbing & HVAC in Bangalore, demonstrating edge case resilience. Notice the red alert: 'Source Disagreement Detected'. The Google listing has one phone number, while their brochure has another. Rather than guessing, we let the owner choose which one to keep with one click.  
  > We also protect against indirect prompt injection in uploaded files by isolating raw data in XML delimiters, and we handle social scraping blocks gracefully."*
